Around midwinter we turn inward. During this special we work from subtle energies and deep layers of your subconscious. This deepens and widens your awareness in the here and now.

You are warmly invited, without obligation, to connect beforehand with the darkest of the dark and with the light in yourself. We look at the shadow sides, the parts we would rather not see, with love, and take them in. And we explore and celebrate the light in you.

**How the day unfolds**

You are welcome from 1.30 pm. We start at 2 pm and finish around 9 pm. During the day we see which constellations need to come forward. We work with a maximum of 20 participants and set up roughly a handful of questions.

Constellations are set up "blind": representatives are invited into the field without knowing who or what they represent. No participant hears the question or its details without the questioner's consent.

**Food and drink**

We provide warming drinks, delicious snacks and a home-cooked, warm organic meal.

**Cancellation**

A constellation day is a dynamic group process in which we depend on each other's presence. We therefore ask you not to cancel at short notice. Should you be unable to come after all, please let us know as soon as possible so we can give your place to someone else.
